This file is a template. Copy it, give the copy a new name and write over this text. The filename becomes the address: `whatsapp-order-questions.md` is published at `/blog/whatsapp-order-questions/`.

While a post has `draft: true` at the top, it shows only when you run the site on your own computer. Remove that line to publish it, then push to `main`.

## Headings start at two hashes

The post's title is the page's main heading, so headings in the text start at `##`. Use them to break a long post into parts a reader can scan.

### A smaller heading

Use `###` for a part within a part.

#### The smallest heading

Use `####` sparingly, for short labelled points.

## Text

A paragraph can carry **bold** for the one phrase that matters, *italic* for a title or a gentle stress, a [link to the contact section](/#contact) and `inline code` for anything typed exactly.

Keep to the site's voice: sentence case, British spelling, "we" and "you", outcome first, and "AI agents" rather than "bots".

## Lists

A bulleted list, for points in no particular order:

- Answers order, stock and price questions
- Drafts replies in your tone
- Passes unusual cases to your team

A numbered list, for steps that happen in order:

1. A customer asks about an order on WhatsApp.
2. The AI agent checks your stock and price list.
3. Your team approves the reply and sends it.

## A quotation

> Your team stays in control. AI agents do the checking, drafting and preparing, and your team approves what matters before it goes out.

## A code block

Wrap anything that must be copied exactly in three backticks:

```
Hello Magnisale, I would like to book a discovery call.
```

## A table

| Task | Before | With an AI agent |
|---|---|---|
| Answering order questions | Typed by hand | Drafted for your team to approve |
| Weekly report | Gathered on Friday afternoon | Prepared before you ask |

## An image

Put images in `public/blog/` in a folder named after the post, and link to them from the site's root. Always describe the image in the square brackets, for people who cannot see it.
